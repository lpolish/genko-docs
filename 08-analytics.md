# Analytics & Insights

Analytics gives you a real-time view of how the practice is performing across appointments, patients, providers, and outcomes such as completion and no-show rates.

---

## Plan access

Analytics is available on **Group** plans and above.

If your practice is still on Free, Solo, or Starter, you can run daily operations normally, but analytics will stay locked until you upgrade.

---

## What the analytics dashboard shows

The Analytics page combines high-level KPIs with breakdowns you can explore in more detail. It is designed for operational insight rather than financial back-office reporting.

At a glance, you can track:

- Appointment volume
- Completion rate
- No-show rate
- Cancelled appointments
- New patient growth
- Provider activity
- Service-level demand patterns

---

## Date ranges and presets

You can analyze performance with:

- **Presets** such as Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, This month, Last month, and This year
- **Custom ranges** when you need to compare specific periods

The dashboard updates as soon as you change the selected range.

---

## Key performance indicators

Genkō surfaces seven core KPIs for the selected date range:

| KPI | What it means |
|-----|----------------|
| Total appointments | All appointments in the period |
| Completed appointments | Visits that were completed |
| No-shows | Appointments marked as no-show |
| Cancelled appointments | Visits cancelled by staff or patient |
| Completion rate | Completed appointments divided by total |
| No-show rate | No-shows divided by total |
| New patients | New patient records created in the period |

---

## Breakdowns and filters

Below the KPIs, you can review the data by:

- **Day** to see trends over time
- **Appointment type** to compare services
- **Provider** to compare workload and outcomes
- **Status** to understand the mix of completed, cancelled, scheduled, and no-show visits

These breakdowns help answer operational questions quickly, such as which provider has the highest completion rate or which service attracts the most no-shows.

---

## Export data

Use **Export** to download analytics in:

- **CSV**
- **Excel**

Exports are helpful when you want to do custom analysis in Google Sheets, Excel, Power BI, or Tableau.

---

## Best practices

- Review analytics weekly, not only when something goes wrong.
- Watch no-show rates after enabling the portal or changing reminder workflows.
- Compare providers and services before adding new schedule rules or staffing.
