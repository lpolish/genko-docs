# Plans & Billing

Genkō pricing is structured so small practices can start simple and unlock more automation, analytics, integrations, and scale as they grow.

---

## Available plans

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Patients | Staff |
|------|---------|--------|----------|-------|
| Free | $0 | — | 100 | 3 |
| Solo | $29 | $290 | 250 | 4 |
| Starter | $49 | $490 | 500 | 6 |
| Group | $89 | $890 | 1,000 | 10 |
| Practice | $199 | $1,990 | 5,000 | 30 |
| Enterprise | $449 | $4,490 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

All plans include core scheduling features such as appointment booking, conflict detection, patient self-service portal access, and provider availability management.

---

## Feature unlocks by plan

- **Solo and above** add multiple services, appointment fees, Google Calendar sync, and Stripe payment collection
- **Group and above** add analytics, WhatsApp messaging, and MCP API access
- **Practice and above** add advanced scheduling rules, the dashboard AI assistant, and AI-powered WhatsApp scheduling
- **Enterprise** adds custom integrations, dedicated support, and SLA options

---

## Upgrading and downgrading

Go to **Settings → Billing** to manage your subscription.

### Upgrade

- Upgrades take effect immediately
- Stripe Checkout handles the purchase flow
- Prorated charges can apply mid-cycle

### Downgrade

- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period
- You keep current access until then
- Make sure your patient and staff counts fit the lower plan before the change takes effect

### Switch billing interval

You can also move between monthly and yearly billing from the same area.

---

## Billing portal

After subscribing, use **Manage Subscription** to open the Stripe billing portal.

From there, authorized users can:

- Update payment methods
- Download invoices and receipts
- Review billing history
- Cancel a subscription while keeping access until the current period ends

Owners and admins can access billing settings.

---

## Usage awareness

Plan limits affect operational workflows such as:

- Adding new patients
- Inviting team members
- Unlocking analytics
- Creating API keys for MCP

Before downgrading, check current usage so you do not end up above the limits of the lower tier.

---

## Best practices

- Choose the plan based on active patients and team size first, then advanced features second.
- Recheck usage before large onboarding pushes.
- Upgrade before you hit a hard operational limit rather than after staff are blocked.
